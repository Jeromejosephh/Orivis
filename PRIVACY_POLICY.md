# Privacy Policy for Orivis

**Last updated:** January 17, 2026

## Overview

Orivis is committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains our practices regarding data collection and use.

## Data Collection

Orivis operates **completely offline**. The application:

- **Does not collect** any personal information
- **Does not transmit** data to servers or cloud services
- **Does not track** user activity or analytics
- **Does not store** images or inspection data outside your device
- **Does not use** advertising networks or third-party trackers

## Permissions

Orivis requests the following device permissions **only** for local on-device processing:

- **Camera:** To capture images for defect detection (local inference only)
- **Photos/Gallery:** To select images from your device (local processing only)

These permissions are used exclusively for local AI model inference and are never shared with external services.

## Data Storage

All data is stored **locally on your device**:
- Inspection history
- Detected defects and confidence scores
- CSV exports
- User settings

You retain full control over this data and can delete it anytime through the app's settings.

## CSV Export

When you export inspection data as CSV, the file is created and stored **only on your device**. You control where to share or store this file.

## No Third-Party Services

Orivis does not integrate with:
- Cloud storage services
- Analytics platforms
- Advertising networks
- Social media APIs
- Any external services

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy. Changes will be noted with an updated "Last updated" date above.
